Where can I be picked up in Downtown Dubai?

Send the complete hotel, residence or business address. Requests can cover Downtown Dubai hotels, residences and business addresses, subject to confirmation.

Which DXB terminal should I provide?

Use the terminal shown in your airline or flight information. Include the airline and flight number so the correct airport destination can be reviewed.

What departure details are needed?

Provide the flight date and time, preferred pickup time, passenger count, luggage and complete pickup address.

How is the vehicle selected?

Vehicle options depend on availability, passenger count and luggage. Include the number and type of bags so a suitable category can be proposed.
