Which airport does AUH refer to?

AUH is the airport code for Zayed International Airport in Abu Dhabi, formerly known as Abu Dhabi International Airport.

Can I request an AUH transfer to Dubai?

Yes. Ciao Mobility accepts private inter-emirate requests from Zayed International Airport to Dubai destinations.

Can you arrange AUH transfers to Yas Island or Abu Dhabi city?

Yes. Provide the exact hotel, residence, venue or business address with the flight details.

What details are needed for an airport pickup?

Send the flight number, arrival date and time, passenger count, luggage, contact number and complete destination.
