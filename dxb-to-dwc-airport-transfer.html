What details are needed for an airport-to-airport transfer?

Send both flight numbers, the arrival and departure dates and times, passenger count, luggage and terminal details.

Allow enough time for immigration, baggage collection, the road journey and onward check-in. Confirm timing with your airlines before booking.

Yes. Share passenger and luggage numbers so a suitable vehicle category can be proposed, subject to availability.

Should the transfer be booked in advance?

Yes. Advance booking is recommended so both airport terminals, timing and vehicle requirements can be confirmed.
