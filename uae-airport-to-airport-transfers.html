FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Plan your airport connection.

Which UAE airports can be included?

Requests can include Dubai International (DXB), Al Maktoum International (DWC), Sharjah International (SHJ) and Zayed International Airport (AUH), subject to confirmation.

What flight information should I provide?

Send both flight numbers, arrival and departure times, terminals, passenger count and luggage.

Can Ciao Mobility guarantee a flight connection?

No. Connection feasibility depends on airline schedules, airport procedures, road conditions and check-in deadlines. Confirm sufficient time with your airlines.

Can a family or group request a private vehicle?

Yes. Share passenger and luggage requirements so a suitable vehicle category can be proposed, subject to availability.
