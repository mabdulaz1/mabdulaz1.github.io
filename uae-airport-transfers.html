COMMON QUESTIONS

UAE airport transfer FAQs.

Which UAE airports can I request a transfer for?

Ciao Mobility accepts private transfer requests involving Dubai International Airport (DXB), Al Maktoum International Airport (DWC), Sharjah International Airport (SHJ) and Zayed International Airport (AUH).

Can I request an airport transfer between different emirates?

Yes. Send the airport, complete destination, passengers, luggage and journey date so the inter-emirate airport transfer can be reviewed.

What details are needed for an airport pickup?

Provide the airline, flight number, arrival date and time, airport or terminal information, complete destination, passenger count and luggage.

Can I request transportation to a UAE airport for departure?

Yes. Include the pickup address, airport, flight departure time, passengers and luggage when requesting a departure transfer.

How do I request a quotation?

Use the website form, WhatsApp, telephone or email and provide the complete journey details. Ciao Mobility will review the request before confirming the transportation.
