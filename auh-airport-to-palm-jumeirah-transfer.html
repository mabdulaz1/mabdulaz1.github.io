PRIVATE UAE JOURNEY

From AUH Terminal A to Palm Jumeirah.

Ciao Mobility arranges private transportation from Zayed International Airport (AUH) to Palm Jumeirah hotels, resorts and residences, including destinations along the trunk, crescent and fronds. COMMON QUESTIONS

AUH to Palm Jumeirah transfer FAQs.

Can I request a private transfer from AUH to Palm Jumeirah?

Yes. Send your flight details, exact Palm Jumeirah destination, passenger count and luggage to request a quotation.

Do I need to provide the full hotel or resort name?

Yes. A complete hotel, resort or residence name helps define the correct destination on Palm Jumeirah.

What details determine the transport requirement?

Provide all passengers, suitcases, oversized luggage and any additional stop or special requirement.

Can I request Palm Jumeirah to Abu Dhabi Airport?

Yes. Share the pickup address, flight departure time and travelling-party details for the reverse journey.
