Before your airport departure.

Can I request pickup from any Dubai hotel or address?

Send the complete hotel, residence or business address. Ciao Mobility will review the pickup location and confirm the transfer arrangements.

Requests can be made for Dubai International Airport (DXB) or Al Maktoum International Airport (DWC). Include the airport and terminal shown on your flight details.

What flight information should I provide?

Provide the airline, flight number, departure date and time, airport and terminal, together with your preferred pickup time.

Vehicle options depend on availability, passenger count and luggage. Include the number and type of bags so a suitable category can be proposed.
