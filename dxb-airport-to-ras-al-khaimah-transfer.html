PRIVATE UAE JOURNEY

From your DXB terminal to Ras Al Khaimah.

Ciao Mobility arranges private transportation from Dubai International Airport to Ras Al Khaimah city, Al Marjan Island, Al Hamra Village, Mina Al Arab and other hotels, resorts or residences. Your request is prepared around the pickup terminal, complete destination, passenger count and luggage requirements.

For an airport departure, you can also request the reverse journey from Ras Al Khaimah to DXB. Share the flight departure time so the pickup requirement can be reviewed.

Details to send

  • Arrival date and flight number
  • DXB terminal, when known
  • Complete Ras Al Khaimah destination
  • Passenger and luggage count
  • Any planned stop or special requirement

COMMON QUESTIONS

Dubai Airport to Ras Al Khaimah transfer FAQs.

Can I request a private transfer from DXB to Ras Al Khaimah?

Yes. Send your flight, destination, passenger and luggage details to request a quotation from Ciao Mobility.

Do you accept resort destinations in Ras Al Khaimah?

You can provide the complete resort or hotel name, including destinations around Al Marjan Island, Al Hamra Village and Mina Al Arab.

What information is needed for an airport pickup?

Share the arrival date, airline, flight number, DXB terminal when known, passenger count, luggage and complete destination.

Can I request Ras Al Khaimah to DXB?

Yes. Send the pickup address, flight departure time, airport terminal and travelling-party details for the reverse journey.
