COMMON QUESTIONS

AUH to Downtown Dubai transfer FAQs.

Can I request a private transfer from AUH to Downtown Dubai?

Yes. Share your AUH arrival details, complete Downtown Dubai destination, passenger count and luggage for a quotation.

Which Abu Dhabi airport terminal is used?

Zayed International Airport operates passenger arrivals through Terminal A. Your airline and flight number help identify the arrival details.

Can I include an additional stop?

Include every requested stop when contacting Ciao Mobility so the complete journey can be reviewed.

Can I book Downtown Dubai to AUH?

You can request the reverse airport journey by sending the pickup address, flight departure time, passenger count and luggage.
